# EcoNexus Nashik — Synthetic Demonstration Dataset Documentation

## Overview & Scientific Provenance Disclaimer

> **IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:**
> All records in this directory (`data/generated/`) are **reproducible synthetic simulation data** created solely for algorithmic development, system integration, routing evaluation, and demonstration purposes for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH).
> 
> - **NOT OFFICIAL MEASUREMENTS:** None of the records represent physical IoT sensor telemetry or official measured historical logs from the Nashik Municipal Corporation (NMC).
> - **APPROXIMATE DEMONSTRATION COORDINATES:** All latitude and longitude coordinates are approximate synthetic centroids distributed across Nashik's 6 municipal wards. They do not represent surveyed physical municipal asset locations.
> - **EXPLICIT PROVENANCE TAGGING:** Every single record in all three files contains the field:
>   `Data_Source = SYNTHETIC_SIMULATION`

---

## Dataset Files & File Structure

This directory contains three core datasets generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_dataset.js`:

### 1. `bin_master_synthetic.csv`
Contains 100 demonstration municipal bin assets distributed across the 6 administrative divisions of Nashik:
- **Panchavati** (`WARD-01`): 20 bins (Market, pilgrimage, and residential clusters around Ramkund, Panchavati Karanja, Tapovan)
- **Nashik West** (`WARD-02`): 18 bins (Commercial arcades and colonies around Canada Corner, College Road, Jehan Circle)
- **Nashik East** (`WARD-03`): 16 bins (Commercial hubs around Shalimar Chowk, Old Agra Road, Dwarka Circle)
- **CIDCO** (`WARD-04`): 18 bins (Dense residential and market clusters around Trimurti Chowk, Pawan Nagar, Pathardi Phata)
- **Satpur** (`WARD-05`): 14 bins (Industrial and transit points around Satpur MIDC ITI and NICE Area)
- **Nashik Road** (`WARD-06`): 14 bins (Transit hubs around Nashik Road Railway Station, Bytco Circle, Jail Road)

**Columns:**
- `Bin_ID`: Standardized alphanumeric identifier (e.g. `BIN-NSK-001` to `BIN-NSK-100`).
- `Area`: Administrative ward name.
- `Ward_Code`: Official ward identifier (`WARD-01` to `WARD-06`).
- `Bin_Name`: Descriptive location name.
- `Bin_Type`: Waste stream classification (`general`, `organic`, `recyclable`, `hazardous`).
- `Area_Category`: Functional zone (`residential`, `commercial`, `market`, `industrial`, `pilgrimage`).
- `Capacity_Litres`: Standard municipal receptacle volume (660L, 1100L, 1200L, 1500L, 2000L, 2400L).
- `Latitude`, `Longitude`: Approximate geospatial coordinates within Nashik municipal bounds.
- `Location_Verification_Status`: `approximate_synthetic`.
- `Data_Source`: `SYNTHETIC_SIMULATION`.

### 2. `bin_daily_history_180_days_synthetic.csv`
Contains 180 consecutive calendar days of daily observations for each of the 100 bins (**exactly 18,000 historical observation records**).

**Daily State Transition Model:**
The time series simulates a continuous physical state transition rather than random numbers:
1. **Initial Fill Level:** Assigned per bin within a valid initial operational baseline.
2. **Daily Accumulation:** Computed from the bin's functional area profile (e.g., market and pilgrimage clusters experience higher weekend generation; commercial clusters experience weekday peaks).
3. **Compactor Collection:** When fill level reaches the collection threshold (>= 70%–85%) or schedule frequency exceeds 3 days, a collection event is executed.
4. **Fill Reduction:** After emptying by a compaction vehicle, fill level resets to 0.0%–5.0% (residual).
5. **Carryover State:** The ending fill percentage carries over into the next morning's state.
6. **Bounds Enforced:** Fill percentage is strictly bounded within `[0.0%, 100.0%]`.

**Columns:**
- `Observation_ID`: Sequential observation key (`OBS-000001` to `OBS-018000`).
- `Date`: ISO calendar date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
- `Bin_ID`: Foreign key to `bin_master_synthetic.csv`.
- `Area`: Administrative ward name.
- `Bin_Type`: Waste classification.
- `Capacity_Litres`: Receptacle capacity.
- `Fill_Percentage`: Bounded fill level between 0% and 100%.
- `Estimated_Waste_Volume_Litres`: `Capacity_Litres * (Fill_Percentage / 100)`.
- `Simulated_Daily_Waste_Accumulation_Litres`: Generated daily volume.
- `Collection_Performed`: Flag (`1` if collected today, `0` otherwise).
- `Collection_Quantity_Litres`: Volume cleared by compactor truck (if collected).
- `Overflow_Risk_Category`: Risk classification based on fill level (`normal` < 50%, `monitor` 50–69%, `high` 70–84%, `critical` >= 85%).
- `Day_Of_Week`: `Sunday` through `Saturday`.
- `Data_Source`: `SYNTHETIC_SIMULATION`.
- `Data_Quality_Note`: Descriptive provenance audit note.

---

### 3. `simulated_collection_events.csv`
Contains the operational ledger of all compactor truck collection events that occurred during the 180-day simulation.

**Columns:**
- `Collection_Event_ID`: Sequential event key (`COL-000001` ...).
- `Date`: Date of collection.
- `Bin_ID`: Receptacle identifier.
- `Fill_Percentage_Before_Collection`: Measured or estimated fill prior to collection.
- `Estimated_Or_Recorded_Collection_Quantity_Litres`: Net volume transferred to compactor truck.
- `Fill_Percentage_After_Collection`: Post-collection residual fill (0%–5%).
- `Event_Status`: `completed`.
- `Data_Source`: `SYNTHETIC_SIMULATION`.

## Validation & Verification

A dedicated Node.js validator is provided at `validate_dataset.js`.
To validate the dataset integrity at any time, run:

```bash
node data/generated/validate_dataset.js
```

The script checks column headers, numeric bounds, date continuity, primary/foreign key integrity, and logical consistency.
